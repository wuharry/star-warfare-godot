No text, labels, logos, watermark, busy scenery or extreme weathering. Colored glass is not emissive; only selected small LED points glow. Do NOT add generic triangular nose shield or standard motorcycle chin bar unless required by the specific identity. The source thumbnails may be unevenly lit; reproduce the intended palette in neutral lighting, not colored shadows.
