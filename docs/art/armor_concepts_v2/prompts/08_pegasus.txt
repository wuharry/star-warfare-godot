Use case: stylized-concept. Generate ONE newly designed armor concept sheet for a compact stylized sci-fi infantry game. Reference image 1 is the old helmet; reference image 2 is the old torso. These references show the character identity to reinterpret, NOT geometry or texture to duplicate. Preserve the distinctive motif named in the brief while making new manufactured plate shapes, fasteners, recesses and articulation. PRIORITY: this suit must be recognizable in grayscale and have a dramatically different helmet and shoulder/chest silhouette from a generic tactical soldier. Do not standardize every helmet into the same black motorcycle visor. Keep compact Star Warfare-like body proportions: oversized helmet relative to a short broad torso, short sturdy legs, large armored gloves and boots, around five helmet-heights overall. Hard metallic armor should be thick separate geometric shells, strong readable facets, small bevels, recessed sensors, raised guard plates, dark flexible joints with motion clearance. Modern painted metal, subtle exposed metallic edges, clean restrained surfaces and very few purposeful LED windows. Shape and primary forms first, details secondary. No unnecessary full-body neon or dense tiny panel lines. Materials and quality consistent across the series: stylized high-quality game-ready-looking 3D render, NOT a photograph of a tall human, NOT a toy, no cloth-like bending armor. Neutral gray studio background with soft key light and visible dark surfaces. Layout: LEFT large full-body front three-quarter view, UPPER RIGHT large helmet closeup, LOWER RIGHT smaller full-body rear three-quarter view. The three views must show the SAME design with consistent structural details and colors, both boots fully visible. Empty hands, relaxed slight A stance, no weapons. No text, labels, logos, watermark, busy scenery or extreme weathering. Colored glass is not emissive; only selected small LED points glow. Do NOT add generic triangular nose shield or standard motorcycle chin bar unless required by the specific identity. The source thumbnails may be unevenly lit; reproduce the intended palette in neutral lighting, not colored shadows.
