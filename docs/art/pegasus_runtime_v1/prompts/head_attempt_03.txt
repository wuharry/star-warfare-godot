Paint design changes only within the actual relevant charts; do not transfer a helmet vent onto an ear or a forehead onto the rear.

Total differences must be measured against this armor's true original glTF source, not against another armor or an intermediate candidate. The allowed ceiling is 20% for original-normalized displacement, per-axis dimensions, gray silhouette difference and changed UV-coordinate fraction per material. Return only the full edited flat opaque square atlas at the target framing, retaining opaque padding and island outlines. No perspective, character render, turnaround, rearranged art sheet, technical lines/colors, captions, text, logos or watermark. Save all raw native generation attempts and copy selected PNG bytes unchanged without post-generation painting, tinting, cropping or resampling.
