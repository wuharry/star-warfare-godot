Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset: C-09 BULWARK; replacement concept for original Star Warfare Pegasus, game armor ID 8.
Reference order: FIRST is the restored original Star Warfare armor. SECOND is Halo 5: Guardians HELIOSKRILL-class Mjolnir (GEN2), a specifically selected non-Master-Chief design. Use both as active visual design inputs. The original dictates color identity and the retained distinguishing cues; the named Halo reference supplies clearly recognizable helmet casing, cheek, collar, torso and articulation construction. Create a new integrated design, not a pasted helmet.
Fusion brief: Fuse the original Star Warfare Pegasus with the observed Halo 5 Helioskrill helmet-and-collar language. Preserve Pegasus's unmistakable red helmet over off-white body armor, narrow dark V-shaped visor, two short brow points, broad pale shoulders, and sparse blue seam accents. Borrow the reference's layered cheek guards, human neck opening, articulated shoulder plates, and angled rib-protecting chest structure. Replace the single ornate swept crown with two much lower divided brow rails that grow out of Pegasus's own short points. Keep the visor continuous glass with a shallow central V; do not turn the face into eyes or a robotic muzzle. Rear structure may combine a newly shaped vertical central cover with two short side rails, informed by the documented Helioskrill rear concept but with a different panel sequence. Maintain normal human hands, soft joints and waist flexibility. Do not copy the purple-gold coating, alien emblems, exact crest or decorative striping.
Priority: a stocky HUMAN wearing futuristic armor, roughly 6 to 6.5 helmet-heights, broad chest, strong thighs, anatomically placed joints. Give the selected Halo helmet traits enough presence to be visible in silhouette, while maintaining the original armor's described identity. Five-finger human gloves, proportional boots, black woven flexible undersuit at neck, elbows, armpits, waist and knees. Large readable armor shells, moderate broad bevels, restrained service details; shorten protrusions for plausible head and arm movement.
Composition: ONE full-body figure in a relaxed near-front three-quarter standing pose, all helmet and soles visible, empty hands, feet grounded. Portrait 1024 x 1536. Pale neutral gray studio background, soft neutral directional light, high-quality painted game concept render, slightly stylized broad surfaces and modest edge wear. No weapons, scenery, captions, labels, insignia, 117, UNSC, or Master Chief's helmet. No skinny proportions, chibi proportions, robot eyes, mechanical face, exposed robotic joints or huge exoskeleton limbs. No separate backpack visible in this front concept; the separately designed B-09 will be fitted only in the later reference sheet, as an optional independent item. Do not reproduce the sources' exact panel outlines or coating graphics.
